Reading an exchange-rate quote
Every quote prices ONE currency in units of ANOTHER (illustrative rate)
US$1
the currency being priced
=
₹80
its price, in rupees
Flip it:
₹1 = US$0.0125
(1 ÷ 80)
If the quote moves from ₹80 to ₹88 per US$
US$ appreciates
One dollar now buys more rupees.
₹ depreciates
One rupee now buys less: US$0.0125 → US$0.0114
A higher number is not automatically a stronger currency. First ask: which currency is the “1”?
