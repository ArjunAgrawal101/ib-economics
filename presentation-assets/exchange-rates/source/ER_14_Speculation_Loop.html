Speculation and expectations
When enough traders act on the same expectation, they can cause the change they expect
Traders expect the ₹ to appreciate
They buy ₹ now to sell later at a profit
Demand for ₹ rises: D shifts right
The ₹ appreciates, confirming the expectation

In reverse: expect a fall → sell ₹ → supply of ₹ rises → ₹ depreciates. Large, fast flows can make rates volatile.
