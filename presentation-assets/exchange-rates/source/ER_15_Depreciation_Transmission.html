How a rupee depreciation spreads through the economy
Two channels work at once; the arrows show likely effects, ceteris paribus
The rupee
depreciates
fewer US$ per ₹
1 · DEMAND CHANNEL
Indian exports cheaper in foreign currency; imports dearer in ₹
→
Exports rise, imports fall (if demand is price elastic)
→
Net exports (X − M) rise
→
AD shifts right
→
Real GDP ↑, unemployment ↓; demand-pull inflation near full capacity
2 · COST CHANNEL
Imported inputs (crude oil, components) cost more in ₹
→
Firms' costs of production rise
→
SRAS shifts left
→
Cost-push inflation; real GDP ↓
Also: debts owed in US$ cost more rupees to repay; households receiving remittances get more ₹ per US$.
Net effect: inflation rises through both channels. Growth and jobs depend on which channel is stronger.
