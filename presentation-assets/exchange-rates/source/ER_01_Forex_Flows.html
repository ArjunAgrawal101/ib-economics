Who buys and who sells rupees?
The foreign exchange market links every cross-border transaction to the demand for, or supply of, a currency
DEMAND for ₹
money flowing into India: someone sells foreign currency to get ₹
1
Foreigners buy Indian exports (goods and services)
2
Inward FDI: foreign firms build or buy in India
3
Inward portfolio: foreigners buy Indian shares and bonds
4
Remittances sent home to India
5
Speculators buy ₹ expecting it to rise
6
RBI buys ₹ using its US$ reserves
→
Foreign exchange market
₹ ⇄ US$
price = exchange rate
←
SUPPLY of ₹
money flowing out of India: someone sells ₹ to get foreign currency
1
Indians buy imports (goods and services)
2
Outward FDI: Indian firms invest abroad
3
Outward portfolio: Indians buy foreign shares and bonds
4
Remittances sent out of India
5
Speculators sell ₹ expecting it to fall
6
RBI sells ₹ to buy US$
