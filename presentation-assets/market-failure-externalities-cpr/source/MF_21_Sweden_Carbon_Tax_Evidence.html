Carbon tax on fossil fuels since 1991
SEK 2501991
SEK 1,5202026 (EUR 138)
nominal SEK per tonne of CO2; 2026 rate for natural gas and coal. Industry in the EU ETS is exempt.
→
Incentive
High-carbon fuels cost more than low-carbon alternatives
Households and firms switch fuels, cut use and invest in cleaner heating and transport.
Tax on a unit of fuel= tax per tonne of CO2 × tonnes of CO2 released by that fuel
→
Evidence
−33%domestic emissions, 1990 to 2021, while real GDP rose 92%
about −11%transport CO2 versus a comparison group on average, 1990 to 2005, with the largest share due to the tax (peer-reviewed study, 2019)
What the evidence cannot show: that the tax caused the whole fall in emissions. The EU ETS, nuclear and hydro power and district heating also played a part.
Sources: Government Offices of Sweden (2026); Ministry of Finance (2023); Andersson, American Economic Journal: Economic Policy (2019)
