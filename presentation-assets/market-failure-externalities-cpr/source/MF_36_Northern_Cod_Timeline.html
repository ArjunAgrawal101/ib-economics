1992
Moratorium
Commercial fishery closed on 2 July; about 30,000 jobs lost in Newfoundland and Labrador
2005
Low point
Spawning stock about 3% of its 1980s average (earlier assessment model)
2024
Reopened
New assessment model and reference point; stock judged out of the critical zone. Canadian TAC 18,000 t
2025
Quota raised
TAC 38,000 t
2026
“Healthy zone”
TAC 59,000 t; first healthy-zone assessment since 1992
A policy decision is not proof of recovery. The zone changed mainly because the reference point was redefined, not because fish numbers suddenly jumped. “Healthy zone” is a model-based category, not a return to pre-collapse abundance.
Sources: Fisheries and Oceans Canada (science advisory reports 2022/041, 2024/046; news releases June 2024 and June 2026; 2025 management decision); The Canadian Encyclopedia
