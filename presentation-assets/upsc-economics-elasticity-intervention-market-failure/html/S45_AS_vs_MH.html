Adverse selection
People who expect high medical bills are keener to buy insurance. If the insurer cannot tell them apart, premiums rise and low-risk buyers leave.
Responses: screening, medical checks, pooled group cover

Moral hazard
Once insured, an owner may take less care of a vehicle; a borrower whose losses are covered may take bigger risks.
Responses: deductibles, co-payments, monitoring
