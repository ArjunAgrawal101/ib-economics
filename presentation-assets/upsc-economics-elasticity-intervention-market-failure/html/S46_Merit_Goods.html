Public good
Defined by
Its characteristics: non-rival and non-excludable
Examples
Street lighting, flood warnings
A technical classification
Merit good
Defined by
A judgement that it is under-consumed if left to private choice
Examples
Education, preventive healthcare
Reasons: information gaps, long-term benefits, externalities
Demerit good
Defined by
A judgement that it is over-consumed if left to private choice
Examples
Tobacco, alcohol
Reasons: harm to the user or to others
Education is a merit good, not a pure public good: classroom seats are rival and fees can exclude.
