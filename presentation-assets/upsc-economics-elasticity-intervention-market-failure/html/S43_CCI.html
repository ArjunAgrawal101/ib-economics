Competition Act, 2002
Competition Commission of India (CCI)
Anti-competitive agreements
Agreements that harm competition, including cartels that fix prices, share markets or rig bids
Abuse of dominance
Being dominant is not illegal. Abusing that position is: for example, unfair prices or shutting out rivals
Combinations
Certain mergers and acquisitions above set thresholds are reviewed before they take effect
Competition advocacy
Promoting awareness of competition and pro-competitive policy
Purpose
Prevent practices that harm competition, promote competition, protect consumer interests and keep markets open to other participants.
