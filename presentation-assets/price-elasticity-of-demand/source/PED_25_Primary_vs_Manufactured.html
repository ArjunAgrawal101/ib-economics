Primary commodity
crude oil · iron ore · copper
buyer: usually a firm buying an input
Manufactured product
laptop · car · watch
buyer: usually a household
SSubstitutes
Very few: little replaces crude oil, iron ore or copper as an input
Many: rival makes and models compete closely
NNecessity
Essential inputs: production stops without them
Lower: an old laptop or car can be kept longer
IShare of spending
Often a small share of the buyer's total production cost
Often a large share of a household's income
TTime to adjust
Buyers need years to redesign processes or switch inputs
Durable: replacement can simply be postponed
Result
|PED| generally below 1: price inelastic
|PED| generally above 1: price elastic
A tendency, not a rule: medicines are manufactured yet usually face price inelastic demand; one brand of rice faces close substitutes on the same shelf.
