T
Time period
More time turns unavailable substitutes into available ones.
Longer period → more price elastic
I
Income proportion
A large share of the budget makes buyers search harder or cut back.
Larger share → more price elastic
N
Necessity
If buyers cannot do without it, they keep buying when the price rises.
More necessary → more price inelastic
Key determinant
S
Substitutes
Can buyers switch?
Number and closeness of alternatives, minus the cost of switching
More and closer substitutes → more price elastic
How easily buyers can switch, delay or do without
