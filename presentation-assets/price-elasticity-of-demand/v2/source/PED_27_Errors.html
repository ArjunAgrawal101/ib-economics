What earns the mark
Calculating
01
✗Writing %ΔP on top
✓Quantity is the numerator: PED = %ΔQD ÷ %ΔP
02
✗Dropping the sign without comment
✓"PED = −0.5; using its magnitude of 0.5, demand is price inelastic"
Wording
03
✗"Elastic means QD falls when price rises"
✓That is the law of demand. Elastic means %ΔQD is proportionately larger than %ΔP
04
✗"The product is elastic"
✓"Demand for the product is price elastic", in a stated market and period
05
✗Quoting one permanent PED
✓PED depends on the buyer, the definition of the market and the time period
Diagrams
06
✗Mixing up shifts and movements
✓A price change moves along D (quantity demanded); other factors shift D (demand)
07
✗"A steep curve is inelastic"
✓Compare slopes only on the same axes and scale; PED changes along a straight line
Revenue and tax
08
✗"A price cut always raises revenue"
✓Only if demand is price elastic over that range
09
✗Treating revenue as profit
✓TR = P × Q. Profit also depends on costs
10
✗"PED decides who pays a tax"
✓Incidence depends on PED relative to PES
