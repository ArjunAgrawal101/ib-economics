The method
Worked example
1
Identify
Pick out income and quantity demanded, old and new.
→
Income $3,000 → $3,300 a month; bus journeys 40 → 38 a month
2
Calculate
Find each % change from the original value.
→
%ΔY = (3,300 − 3,000) ÷ 3,000 × 100 = +10%; %ΔQd = (38 − 40) ÷ 40 × 100 = −5%
3
Compute
Divide: quantity demanded on top.
→
YED = −5% ÷ +10% = −0.5
4
Sign
Positive: normal. Negative: inferior.
→
Negative, so bus travel is an inferior good for this household
5
Magnitude
Say how strong the response is.
→
Qd falls by about 0.5% for each 1% rise in income
6
Mechanism
Explain why buyers respond.
→
Higher income lets the household switch to cars or ride-hailing
7
Qualify
State the assumptions and limits.
→
For this household, over this income range, ceteris paribus; YED may differ at other incomes
