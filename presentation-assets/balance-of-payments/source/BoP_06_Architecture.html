Current account
trade and income flows

merchandise exports and imports
Trade in services

Income
wages, interest, profits, dividends
Current transfers

Capital account

Usually very small
Capital transfers

Non-produced, non-financial assets

Financial account

Can be large and volatile
Foreign direct investment
lasting stakes with influence or control
Portfolio investment
shares and bonds
Reserve assets
central bank's foreign currency and gold
Official borrowing
