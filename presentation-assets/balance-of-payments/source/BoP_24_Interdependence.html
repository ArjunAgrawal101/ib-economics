IF THE CURRENT ACCOUNT IS IN DEFICIT
Current account deficit
India spends more abroad than it earns: it uses more output than it produces
▶
Extra foreign currency needed
to pay for the excess of imports
▶
Financial account surplus
FDI and portfolio inflows, official borrowing, or reserves run down
▶
More foreign claims on India
foreign-owned assets and external debt rise
IF THE CURRENT ACCOUNT IS IN SURPLUS
Current account surplus
earns more abroad than it spends: produces more than it uses
▶
Extra foreign currency earned
from the excess of exports
▶
Financial account deficit
investing and lending abroad, or adding to reserves
▶
More claims on the rest of the world
assets owned abroad rise
Identity, not a cause. The identity says the totals must match. It does not say which comes first: a deficit can attract financing, and strong capital inflows can raise the ₹ and widen the deficit.
