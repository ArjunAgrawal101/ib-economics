Persistent current account deficit possible implications
Exchange rate
More ₹ supplied to pay for imports → downward pressure → dearer imports and imported inflation
Interest rates
The central bank may raise rates to attract inflows → dearer borrowing, slower AD
Foreign ownership
Assets sold to foreigners to finance it → future profits flow out as income debits
Debt
Borrowing from abroad → interest payments (income debits) → debt can snowball
Credit ratings
Rising external debt can lower ratings → higher interest on future loans
Demand management
Pressure for contractionary fiscal or monetary policy to cut imports
Economic growth
Slower if policy tightens or confidence falls; can be faster if the deficit funds productive investment
Less worrying when it is small and stable, financed by long-term FDI, and funds investment in capacity
More worrying when it is large and rising, financed by short-term borrowing or 'hot money', and funds consumption
