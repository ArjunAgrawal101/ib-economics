The learning lab

Labs in which the reader moves a parameter, predicts what will happen, and watches the model answer. Each one is built from stated equations, so the diagram and the numbers cannot disagree.

Type
Interactive
Format
Interactive simulations in the browser
Level
SL and HL
For
Students, Teachers
Topics
Microeconomics, Macroeconomics, Diagrams, Elasticity

What it helps learners do

  • Predict the effect of a change before seeing it
  • Connect a diagram to the numbers it represents
  • See which conclusions depend on the assumptions of the model

The thinking behind it

Prediction first. The lab asks what will happen before it shows the answer, because a wrong prediction that is corrected is remembered better than a right answer that was only read.

What the complete resource contains

  • Market lab: shifts, movements, shortage and surplus
  • Elasticity lab: PED, its class and what happens to revenue
  • AD-AS lab: output gaps and the effects of a shift
  • Policy simulator, What if? and the Decision room
  • A diagram atlas and a diagram builder
