Economics in 60 Seconds

A series of short carousels, each telling one big economic idea as a story in ten pages, from the market for lemons to the sunk cost fallacy. Every issue is linked to the syllabus and has its own retrieval questions on the platform.

Checked
15 October 2026. All ten issues are public on the platform and were reviewed page by page when they were added.

What it helps learners do

  • Understand one big idea quickly, through a story
  • Connect it to the part of the syllabus where it is assessed
  • Test the idea with retrieval questions

The thinking behind it

One idea per issue, told through a familiar situation before it is named. Each issue ends by handing the idea back to the reader as a question.

What the complete resource contains

  • Issue 001: The Market for Lemons (Information asymmetry)
  • Issue 002: Comparative Advantage (Opportunity cost and trade)
  • Issue 003: The Prisoner's Dilemma (Strategic interaction)
  • Issue 004: The Tragedy of the Commons (Common pool resources)
  • Issue 005: Opportunity Cost (Scarcity and choice)
  • Issue 006: The Power to Set Your Price (Elasticity and market power)
  • Issue 007: The Bill Someone Else Pays (Externalities)
  • Issue 008: The Margin (Marginal analysis)
  • Issue 009: Incentives (Prices, signals and behaviour)
  • Issue 010: The Sunk Cost Fallacy (Decision-making)
