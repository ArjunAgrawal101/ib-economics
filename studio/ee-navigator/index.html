The Extended Essay Navigator

An independent interactive guide to the Extended Essay for first assessment in 2027. It lets a student, a supervisor or a coordinator start from where they are and move through the framework, the criteria, research questions and supervision.

Type
Interactive
Format
Interactive web application
Level
All Diploma Programme subjects, with subject notes for Economics, Global Politics and Business Management
For
Students, EE supervisors, Coordinators
Topics
Extended essay, Research, Supervision, Academic integrity

What it helps learners do

  • Find a way into the Extended Essay from your own starting point
  • Understand the assessment criteria as questions asked about the essay
  • Develop a research question that is clear, focused and arguable
  • Distinguish methodology from research methods
  • Plan supervision, reflection and academic integrity

The thinking behind it

Built around roles rather than chapters: choosing a role reorders what the Navigator recommends first, and nothing is hidden from anyone. What the guide states is kept separate from professional interpretation, and where the guide is silent the Navigator says so.

What the complete resource contains

  • Several ways in, depending on where you are
  • The journey from interest to submission, step by step
  • What changed for 2027, and a filter for every change
  • Choosing between the pathways
  • Positioning an inquiry, and research questions that are clear, focused and arguable
  • The criteria explained as questions about the essay
  • Academic integrity and AI: scenarios rated green, amber or red, and where the guide is silent
  • Supervision: what may be said about a draft, and what crosses the line
  • Supervisor allocation, reflection timeline and support plans
  • Interpretation for Economics, Global Politics and Business Management, labelled as professional interpretation rather than IB policy
  • A professional-development mode: twelve modules with facilitator notes

The Navigator is a private application and is not hosted on this site. For a school, a walkthrough can be arranged on request. The Economics EE Studio on this platform is the public, Economics-only counterpart.
